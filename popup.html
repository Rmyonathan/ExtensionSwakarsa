👕 Social Media Scraper

How to use this extension

  1. Click "Open Dashboard" to open the scraping interface
  2. Enter the subreddits you want to scrape
  3. Add any keywords to filter results (optional)
  4. Set the maximum number of posts to scrape from each subreddit
  5. Click "Start Scraping" and wait for results
  6. View and export your results from the dashboard
